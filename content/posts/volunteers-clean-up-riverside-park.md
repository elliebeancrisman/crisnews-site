More than 60 volunteers turned out on Saturday for the fall Riverside Park
cleanup, collecting an estimated **400 pounds of trash** from a one-mile
stretch of riverbank.

Teams worked from 8 a.m. until noon, filling bags with bottles, cans, plastic
wrappers and — in one surprising case — an entire shopping cart.

## Why it matters

The river feeds into the wetlands south of town, where birds and fish are
sensitive to plastic waste. Organizers say regular cleanups make a measurable
difference.

## Want to help next time?

The next cleanup is planned for **April**. Gloves, bags and grabbers are
provided; just bring water and closed-toe shoes.

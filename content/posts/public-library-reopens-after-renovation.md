After six months behind construction fencing, the public library opened its
doors again on Monday morning to a line of eager readers that stretched down
the block.

## A brighter, bigger space

The renovation replaced the old ceiling tiles with skylights, doubled the
number of seats in the main reading room, and added a dedicated **teen space**
with study tables and charging stations.

Children's librarian staff say the new story-time room is already booked for
weekly sessions through December.

## New hours

The library is now open later on weekends:

| Day | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Monday – Friday | 9 a.m. – 8 p.m. |
| Saturday | 9 a.m. – 6 p.m. |
| Sunday | 12 p.m. – 5 p.m. |

Library cards are free for all residents. Bring a photo ID and something with
your address on it to sign up at the front desk.

The Saturday Farmers Market is back on Main Street, and this year it is bigger
than ever. Organizers say **30 new vendors** have signed up for the fall season,
bringing the total to more than 70 stalls every weekend.

## What's new this year

Shoppers will find the usual favorites — fresh vegetables, honey, eggs and
baked goods — along with a few new additions:

- A **kids' corner** with face painting and craft tables from 9 to 11 a.m.
- **Live music** from local bands every other Saturday.
- A **community table** where neighborhood groups can share information.

"We heard from a lot of families that they wanted more to do while they shop,"
said one of the market's volunteer coordinators. "So we made room for it."

## When and where

The market runs **every Saturday from 8 a.m. to 1 p.m.** through the end of
November, rain or shine. Street parking is free before 10 a.m., and the public
lot behind the library is open all morning.

> Bring your own bags — several vendors are offering a small discount if you do.

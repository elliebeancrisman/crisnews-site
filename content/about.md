**CrisNews Network** is a small, independent community news site. We cover the
things that matter close to home: local events, small businesses, schools,
parks, and the people who make our neighborhood what it is.

## What we publish

- **Community news** — openings, closings, meetings and changes around town.
- **Events** — markets, fundraisers, festivals and things to do this weekend.
- **People** — profiles of neighbors doing interesting work.

## Get in touch

Have a story idea, a correction, or a photo to share? Send us an email at
**hello@crisnews-network.online** — we read everything.

## Comments

Most stories have a comment section at the bottom. You can comment with just a
name, or sign in with your email address. Please keep it kind — comments that
are abusive or spam will be removed.
